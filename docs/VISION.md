Outside LMS

- https://datesheet.vu.edu.pk/ - auth — Date Sheet. Creation available around 8–14 days prior to exam start. Late setup (last week) carries a FEE as punishment. Campus selection is completely the student's decision; papers are then arranged on dates/time slots on the same day — total exam days 12 to 16. Rescheduling of missed/unplanned papers: around 4 to 7 days after normal exams end; a missed paper can be arranged within 24 hours of the missed scheduled date. All rescheduled papers are arranged on a single day with multiple time slots — the university allocates/plans it. Exam results are published around 12 to 16 days after exams end. The university first updates CGPA and courses studied; later the complete individual subject scores and grades. The VU Campus with whom the student is registered as ON-CAMPUS can retrieve complete results by the time initial results are published (10 AM to 12 PM on results day); the student grade book updates a day after initial results.
  - Recommended to be an ON-CAMPUS student as the student is facilitated by the campus in case of troubles; otherwise home-based students solve problems on their own with the university. Attending classes at campus or home is NOT completely compulsory in either status.
